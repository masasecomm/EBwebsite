About us

Broadcasting shouldn't be hard.
So we made it easy.

Easy Broadcast started with a simple observation: some of the most powerful voices out there — pastors delivering Sunday messages, coaches sharing hard-won wisdom, podcasters building real community — were sitting on YouTube's sidelines. Not because their content wasn't good enough, but because turning audio into video felt like a job for professionals, big budgets, and hours nobody had to spare.

We didn't think that was fair. A sermon that changes someone's week shouldn't lose its audience just because it was never uploaded. A podcast episode that took hours to record shouldn't earn nothing just because editing video got pushed to the bottom of the to-do list, week after week.

So we built Easy Broadcast to remove that barrier completely. Send us your audio. We turn it into a polished, branded, YouTube-ready video — automatically, in minutes, with no editing software and no learning curve. What used to take a production team now takes a single message.

Our mission is simple: help the people already doing the hard work of creating get the reach and revenue their message deserves. You focus on what you do best. We handle turning it into something the world can find, watch, and support.
